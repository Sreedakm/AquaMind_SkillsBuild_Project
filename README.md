# 🌍 AquaMind – Multi-Agent AI for Global Water Stress Assessment

<p align="center">
  <b>An AI-powered Decision Support System for analyzing global water stress using multiple AI agents.</b><br>
  Supporting <b>UN Sustainable Development Goal 6 (Clean Water & Sanitation)</b>.
</p>

---

## 📖 Overview

AquaMind is a **Multi-Agent AI Decision Support System** that analyzes historical global water stress data, explains water scarcity conditions, recommends sustainable mitigation strategies, and generates concise reports using **IBM Granite** and **n8n**.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🌍 Country-wise water stress analysis
- 📈 Historical trend analysis
- 🤖 Multi-Agent AI architecture
- 💡 AI-generated explanations
- 🌱 Sustainable water management recommendations
- 📄 Automated report generation

---

                 User

## 📂 Dataset

**Global Water Stress & Scarcity Index (1990–Present)**

The dataset contains country-level freshwater indicators including:

- Water Stress Percentage
- Renewable Freshwater Resources
- Freshwater per Capita
- Drinking Water Access
- Agricultural Water Withdrawal
- Water Scarcity Classification
- Historical Water Stress Trends

---

## 🎯 Objective

Develop a **Multi-Agent AI system** that retrieves country-specific water data, analyzes historical trends, explains water stress conditions, recommends sustainable mitigation strategies, and generates an easy-to-understand decision-support report.
